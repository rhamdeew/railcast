## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- Emoji search understands Russian slang from the macOS emoji index, for example "хз" finds 🤷.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- Launcher panel on Option-Space with a searchable list of functions.
- Emoji and symbol search in English and Russian, with recent items first.
- English and Russian translation through Google Translate, with the direction detected automatically.
- Translation requests wait until typing stops and reuse earlier results.
